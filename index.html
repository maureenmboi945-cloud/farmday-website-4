A GOOD START ON THE FARM

COW SOURCING & DELIVERY

Start with
a careful arrival.

Planning to bring a cow to your farm? Prepare for a smoother transition by confirming the animal’s details, transport arrangements and arrival care ahead of time.

  • Record the cow’s name or tag, breed and date of birth.
  • Note the delivery date and any health information provided.
  • Give the animal time to settle into its new surroundings.

MILK PRODUCTION

Know what
each cow produces.

Regular milk records can help you notice changes and understand herd output over time. Farmday lets you enter a cow’s daily yield and see the combined daily total.

  • Record yield in litres per day for each animal.
  • Compare individual records with the herd total.
  • Keep health and feeding notes alongside production.

GOOD FEED, STEADY CARE

FEEDING & DAILY CARE

A steady feed
plan makes a difference.

Keep feeding notes with each cow so you can see what is offered each day. Needs vary with the animal, available forage and milk production, so build a balanced plan with a local livestock adviser.

  • Note the forage and supplements offered.
  • Keep clean water available.
  • Track feed quantities and changes in the herd register.
